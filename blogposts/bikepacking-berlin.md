In September 2025, I cycled from the Netherlands to Berlin. It was a tough week, but the freedom of being on the bike made it all worth it.

![On the road](https://firebasestorage.googleapis.com/v0/b/arnout-toonra.appspot.com/o/arnout-photos%2Fbikepack-berlin%2F1dwKVCaGkeybOD9-HqUZEvtU_O8RGpFRzaI6OOsaFFs-2048x946.jpg?alt=media&token=4f22258d-19e0-44f7-9313-0c82f74d7860#height=350)

## My Setup

- **Bike:** Canyon Endurace 7 Raw
- **Tires:** Schwalbe Pro R 35mm
- **Navigation:** Garmin Edge 540
- **Gear:** Ortlieb & Restrap bags, Decathlon Simond MT 900 1-person tent

## The Route

I followed the "Fietsen naar Berlijn" route ([link](https://www.recreatief-fietsen.nl/fietsennaarberlijn/)). It's a beautiful route that avoids busy roads.

## The Trip

### Day 1: Start
**43.5 km | 1h 57m**
Short day to start. Just warming up and checking if everything on the bike was working right.
[View on Strava](https://www.strava.com/activities/15797432987)

### Day 2: Hills and Woods
**90.2 km | 4h 50m**
Tough day! Lots of climbing and riding through the woods. The gravel parts were tricky but fun.
[View on Strava](https://www.strava.com/activities/15810803690)

![Rainy ride](https://firebasestorage.googleapis.com/v0/b/arnout-toonra.appspot.com/o/arnout-photos%2Fbikepack-berlin%2FGzFoTC8LVSD2ON52ILcSA4EaQQ5JL9Gd3wFhsFI77-M-946x2048.jpg?alt=media&token=1c94d2a2-47f8-4e5e-a987-8fcf065001c9#height=550)

### Day 3: Long Day
**119.3 km | 5h 17m**
Long day in the saddle. We got lost a bit, but still a good ride.
[View on Strava](https://www.strava.com/activities/15820737905)

![Trip photo](https://firebasestorage.googleapis.com/v0/b/arnout-toonra.appspot.com/o/arnout-photos%2Fbikepack-berlin%2FTVfcro38NDHa4Ha6YxQoN25ABXncp3YlHM9vDfILIPU-946x2048.jpg?alt=media&token=eccb2388-1450-4fdb-a5a0-f9530d5fabc9#height=550)

### Day 4: The Big One
**154.2 km | 6h 50m**
Over 150km. We had sun, rain, wind, and even a flat tire. But we saw some cool stuff along the way.
[View on Strava](https://www.strava.com/activities/15833937770)

### Day 5: Tailwind
**122.0 km | 5h 35m**
Nice day with the wind in our back. Just cruising from breakfast to dinner.
[View on Strava](https://www.strava.com/activities/15844198326)

### Day 6: Almost There
**119.5 km | 5h 15m**
Solid day of riding. Only 120km left to Berlin!
[View on Strava](https://www.strava.com/activities/15855205374)

![On the way](https://firebasestorage.googleapis.com/v0/b/arnout-toonra.appspot.com/o/arnout-photos%2Fbikepack-berlin%2FghrB6G03X-vYqOfj1A0luXuK6E0aDgNFAgbeBWZVW9Q-2048x946.jpg?alt=media&token=dc63b8c8-bc74-4d08-bfde-e37bb35e1cb2#height=450)

### Day 7: Reaching Berlin
**96.7 km | 4h 29m**
We reached the area around Berlin. Rode with another bikepacker today, weather was perfect.
[View on Strava](https://www.strava.com/activities/15866064875)

### Arrival
**36.0 km | 2h 04m**
Made it! 800km total. Riding into the city felt amazing.
[View on Strava](https://www.strava.com/activities/15876036039)
